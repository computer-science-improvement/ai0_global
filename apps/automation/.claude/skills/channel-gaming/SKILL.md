# Gaming Channel

## Audience
Readers are gamers. They know what Steam, Epic, DLC, Early Access, roguelike mean.
Do NOT explain gaming concepts. Do NOT add "for gamers" or "for those who enjoy games".
Treat the reader as someone who already plays.

## Names and terms
Game titles, studio names, platform names — keep in original (do not translate or transliterate).
Ukrainian verbs and structure, English proper nouns.
- Good: "Студія Larian випустила патч" / "Гра доступна в Steam"
- Bad: "Стім", "Епік Геймс", "Ладдер оф Чейнс"

## Deals and giveaways — be direct
Lead with the concrete fact: what is free / how much off / on what platform.
Price info goes in the first or second sentence, not at the end.
Do NOT use "ви можете отримати", "є можливість", "пропонується" — just state the fact.
- Bad: "У Steam є можливість придбати гру зі знижкою 75%"
- Good: "Hollow Knight коштує $1.24 в Steam — знижка 75%."

## News — name who did what
- Bad: "компанія анонсувала гру" → Good: "Capcom анонсувала Monster Hunter Wilds"
- Bad: "гравці отримають контент" → Good: "гравці отримають безкоштовне DLC у квітні"

## Length
Giveaway / deal posts: 3–5 sentences max. Shorter is better.
News posts: up to 6 sentences. One fact per sentence.
