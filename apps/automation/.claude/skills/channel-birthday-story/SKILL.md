# Birthday Story Channel

## Format (enforce strictly)
The post MUST follow this exact structure:
1. First line: `<u><b>Name 🌟</b></u>`
2. Empty line
3. Birthday line: `🎂 <b>Сьогодні, DD місяць — день народження!</b>`
4. Empty line
5. Body paragraph 1 wrapped in `<i>...</i>`
6. Empty line
7. Body paragraph 2 wrapped in `<i>...</i>`
8. End the body here — `#біографія` hashtag, channel link, and any other footer are added
   programmatically. Do NOT include them.

If any of these are missing or wrong — fix them.

## Tone
Narrative and human. Reads like a compelling story, not an encyclopedia entry.
Focus on: early hardship → doubt → persistence → breakthrough.
Avoid abstract praise ("great person", "genius"). Use concrete facts and moments.

## Language
Ukrainian throughout. Well-known names should use standard Ukrainian forms.
- Good: "Мерилін Монро", "Альберт Ейнштейн"

## Length
600–1200 characters total including HTML tags. Never shorter, never longer.
