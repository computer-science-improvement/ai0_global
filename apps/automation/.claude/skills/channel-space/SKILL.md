# Space Channel

## Audience
Readers are Ukrainian space and science enthusiasts. They follow launches, missions, and discoveries.
Do NOT over-explain basic concepts like orbit, gravity, or light-year.
Make complex topics accessible without dumbing down. One fact per sentence.

## Names and terms
Mission names (Artemis, James Webb, Starship), agency names (NASA, ESA, JAXA, SpaceX, Blue Origin),
spacecraft names (Dragon, Starliner, Orion) — keep in original. Do not transliterate.
Ukrainian verbs and sentence structure, English proper nouns.
- Good: "NASA запустила зонд Europa Clipper"
- Bad: "НАСА запустила зонд Юропа Кліппер"

## Scientific terms
Translate scientific terms accurately. Use established Ukrainian equivalents:
- чорна діра (not "блек хол")
- екзопланета, нейтронна зірка, гравітаційні хвилі
- телескоп, орбіта, місія, модуль

When no established Ukrainian term exists, use the English term with a brief explanation on first
mention.

## Structure
Lead with the main event or discovery. Put the most important fact first.
Follow with context: who, when, what instrument or mission.
End with significance or next steps if relevant.

## Length
Posts: up to 6 sentences. One fact per sentence. Keep total post under 900 characters.
