# ai0-news Channel

## Audience
Ukrainian readers interested in tech, science, energy, and sports.
Educated general audience — no need to over-explain, but avoid jargon without context.

## Tone
Journalistic and neutral. Report facts, avoid hype or sensationalism.
Do not use promotional language ("revolutionary", "game-changing", "unprecedented").

## Language
Ukrainian throughout. Proper nouns stay in original form.
- Good: "Apple випустила iOS 18" / "Elon Musk заявив"
- Bad: "Еппл", "Елон Маск"

## Structure
One clear fact per sentence. Short paragraphs. No filler.

## Tag (single hashtag per post)
Exactly ONE tag per post, chosen from the primary subject of the article — not adjacent context.

Allowed tag vocabulary:
- `tech` — IT, software, hardware, AI, gadgets
- `science` — research, biology, physics, medicine (as research)
- `space` — astronomy, rockets, space missions
- `energy` — power grids, nuclear, oil, renewables
- `crime` — murders, robberies, convictions, courts ruling on crime
- `world` — international politics, conflicts, non-UA geopolitics
- `ukraine` — domestic UA news
- `sports` — competitions, athletes, results
- `business` — markets, M&A, corporate news (non-tech)

Classification rule: pick the tag that matches what the article is ABOUT, not a random prop in
the story.
- Article about a UK court convicting teenagers for murder → `crime`, NOT `health` (even if the
  weapon was an air rifle) and NOT `world` (crime is more specific).
- Article about a new AI model released by Google → `tech`, NOT `business`.
- Article about SpaceX launching a satellite → `space`, NOT `tech`.
- Rule of thumb: if the headline removed the subject, what category of news website would this
  belong on? That's the tag.
