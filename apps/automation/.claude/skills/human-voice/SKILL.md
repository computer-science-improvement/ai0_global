# Human Voice — Ukrainian

## Name the actor, use active verbs
Every sentence needs a subject doing something. Prefer strong concrete verbs over nominalizations.
- Bad: "відбулось підписання угоди" → Fix: "компанії підписали угоду"
- Bad: "здійснено виплату" → Fix: "виплатили"
- Bad: "прийнято рішення" → Fix: "[хто] вирішив"

## Specifics over abstractions
Numbers, names, dates, places — always over vague descriptions.
- Bad: "значна сума" → Fix: "$8 млн"
- Bad: "через деякий час" → Fix: "через три роки" / "у вівторок"
- Bad: "ряд компаній" → Fix: "Apple, Google і Meta" або "п'ять компаній"

If the source doesn't give a specific number — don't invent one, omit the claim.

## Sentence rhythm
Mix sentence lengths naturally: one short, one or two medium, repeat.
- Short sentence = fact delivery. Medium sentence = context or consequence.
- Don't write three sentences of the same length in a row.
- The first sentence of the body should be the heaviest fact, not a setup.

## Connectors that sound human
Use: але, а, хоча, втім, при цьому, водночас, тому, через те що, після того як
Avoid: таким чином, відповідно до цього, у зв'язку з вищесказаним, зважаючи на все вищезазначене

## Tone
Write as an informed person explaining something to a friend who missed the news.
Neutral and direct. Not excited. Not cautious. Not journalistic.
If the news is bad — say it plainly. If it's absurd — the facts speak for themselves, no commentary needed.
Never interpret, judge, or speculate. State what happened.

## The "so what" is implied, never stated
Don't write "це означає що…" or "наслідком стане…" unless it's a direct quote from the source.
Readers draw conclusions themselves. Your job is to give them the facts clearly.
