# On This Day Channel

## Audience
Readers are Ukrainians interested in history. They appreciate concise, engaging historical facts.
Write as someone sharing interesting discoveries, not as a textbook.

## Names and terms
Translate names where a standard Ukrainian translation exists.
- Good: "Альберт Ейнштейн", "Ісаак Ньютон", "Вільям Шекспір", "Наполеон Бонапарт"

Keep less-known names in original if no established Ukrainian form exists.
- Good: "Tim Berners-Lee", "Ada Lovelace"

## Month names — always Ukrainian
Use Ukrainian month names: січня, лютого, березня, квітня, травня, червня, липня, серпня, вересня,
жовтня, листопада, грудня.

## Style
Each event is a short paragraph: year + what happened.
Keep it informative and concise. One fact per event, no padding.
Do not add commentary, opinions, or "fun fact" framing.

## Length
Total post must fit within 900 characters.
Prioritize events over births if space is tight.
